Chickefrain asks for no permissions. No system permission prompt appears, and there is no usage-description string.

There is no login, no in-app purchase, no user-generated content shared with other people, no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. "Delete library" removes books, lines, words, seated days, and page progress from this device only. There is no account to delete.

Chickefrain is a private reading notebook for someone who wants to keep a line after the book is finished. You save books, write lines from them, and attach one word to a line. When the same word sits on three different books, Weave seats three of those lines for the day. No credentials or demo account are needed.

On a cold launch the system launch screen appears, then the home screen titled "Quotes" while the library loads. On a first launch the intro replaces that home screen. It has three pages and page dots. Page 1 is titled "Keep the line" with the body "Save a line from a book you finished. It stays after the book is gone." and buttons "Next" and "Skip". "Next" opens page 2, titled "Weave today", body "When one word sits on three books, Weave seats three lines for this day.", buttons "Next" and "Skip". "Next" opens page 3, titled "Reopen the day", body "Reopen if you want a different three. The word stays on each line.", and the button "Continue". "Skip" is not shown on page 3. "Skip" or "Continue" leaves the intro and opens "Quotes".

On "Quotes", tap "Shelf", then "Add a book". Fill "Title" and "Author", and "Pages" (placeholder "0"). Tap "Save book". Open the book. "Reread" and "Once" switch the mark. "Previous page" and "Next page" change the page. "Mark gone" asks "Mark this book gone?" with "This book leaves the held shelf. Lines stay and can still be woven." Buttons are "Mark gone" and "Cancel". After that the button reads "Gone. Lines remain." Return to "Quotes" and tap "Add line". If the screen says "Add a book before a line.", add a book from "Shelf" first. Otherwise choose "Book", type "Line", optionally "Page" (placeholder "Optional") and "Word", then tap "Save line". The note under Word says "A word is saved on this line. Three books with the same word make Weave live." Tap a line card to open "Word". It shows "Save a word" and "Three books that share it make Weave live for today." Type a word and tap "Save word". Put the same word on lines from three different books. Several lines in one book still count as one book. On "Quotes", when the status reads "Ready. Tap Weave to seat these three lines.", tap "Weave". The heading becomes "Seated from three books" and the status reads "Seated for today. Tap Reopen to choose again." "Reopen" clears today's three seated lines. The words stay on the lines, and "Weave" can be used again. Tap "Days" for today's date and the chips "Empty", "Waiting", "Ready", or "Seated". "Show lines" expands the day. "Weave this day" seats that day. "Reopen this day" clears that day's seated lines. Tap "Counts". A row is the word and a count such as "3 lines". Tap a word, then "Use [word] on Quotes". Tap "Settings" for the summary, "Export", "Share summary", "Contact", "Show intro again", and "Delete library". "Share summary" opens the system share sheet. "Show intro again" shows the three intro pages again and does not clear the library. "Delete library" asks "Delete the library?" with "Books and lines are removed from this device." Buttons are "Delete library" and "Cancel". The Apple Watch app is also named "Chickefrain" and does not edit the phone library. Its usual screen says "Ready", "Weave", and "Seat three lines from three books." A screen titled "Shelf" lists "The Waves", "Middlemarch", and "The Odyssey". A counts screen shows "threshold", "3 lines", and "Days in a row wait until Weave." Those watch lines are fixed.

Books, lines, words, and seated days stay on this device. "Share summary" sends only the one-line count sentence through the system share sheet.
